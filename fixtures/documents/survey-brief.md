# Synthetic survey brief

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Every place, vessel and value below is invented for
software testing and describes no real operation or programme.

- Area: fictional approach channel off Port Example North.
- Method: multibeam bathymetry along twelve parallel lines, 50 m spacing.
- After the survey, the buoy tender lays three sensor buoys at the positions in
  `buoy-positions.csv`; the survey vessel then verifies them.
- Separately, the tug recovers a test mooring near Port Example South.
